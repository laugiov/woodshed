# The awake videos

`awake.mp4` and `awake.webm`: two seconds of 16x16 black frames, no sound, made by woodshed's own
`scripts/awake_video.py` with ffmpeg. Played muted in a loop, they keep a phone's screen on where the
browser has no Screen Wake Lock (`core/awake.js`). Under woodshed's licence (MIT, see the repository's
LICENSE).
