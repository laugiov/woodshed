# Demo song

**"As I Am"** by **Josh Woodward**, from the album *The Shade from Our Trees* (2019).

- Song page, with its lyrics: https://www.joshwoodward.com/song/AsIAm
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Files: `As I Am.mp3` is the recording as published (unchanged, from
  https://archive.org/details/JoshWoodward-TheShadeFromOurTrees); `As I Am.txt` is the lyrics as
  published on the song page (unchanged).

They ship with woodshed so that anyone who clones the repository has a song to try it on:

```bash
woodshed "demo/As I Am.mp3"
woodshed lyrics "demo/As I Am.woodshed" "demo/As I Am.txt"
```

The practice page is built next to the song (`demo/As I Am.woodshed/`, not tracked by git).

Thank you, Josh, for making your music free to use.
