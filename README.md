# woodshed

Turn any song file into a local practice room.

`woodshed song.mp3` analyses the recording on your computer and opens a practice page:
- the chord chart;
- a part for guitar, bass, keys, drums and voice;
- loops, tempo control and play-along.

Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.

Status: early development. Nothing to install yet.

Licence: MIT.

Models: the songs are analysed on your computer by models downloaded once. Placing lyrics uses
MMS, a speech model by Meta, under CC-BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial use). You are responsible for the
lyrics you paste: they stay in your library.
